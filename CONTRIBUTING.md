# Contribution guidelines

A contribution should increase **navigability, teaching quality, or evidence quality**, not merely repository size.

Required source card fields: stable `id`, exact `title`, `publisher`, canonical `url`, `country`, `domain`, `summary` in original words, `source_type`, `time_scope`, `rights`, `review_status`, `added_at`.

Required claim fields: stable `id`, bounded claim, `source_ids`, claim type, evidence status, date and limitations.

Every significant change should include: corrected links, evidence-class label, what changed, how to reproduce the source finding, and whether the source has been independently checked. Do not upload copyrighted full articles as a shortcut. Mark speculation as speculation, not established knowledge.

Country coverage is expandable; do not infer that a lack of publicly accessible evidence means a country lacks a capability. Do not publish specific targeting-relevant operational details.
