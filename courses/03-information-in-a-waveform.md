# Lesson 03 — Information in a waveform

**Learning objective:** Describe AM, FM, digital symbols and how information changes observable waveforms.

**Pre-class reading:** [docs/spectrum/02_signal_families.md](../docs/spectrum/02_signal_families.md)

## Core concepts
Read the pre-class chapter and identify three carefully defined terms. Check them against the official sources, rather than a model-generated summary. Distinguish an objective physical principle from an institutional claim or legal assertion.

## Recommended sources
- [SRC-005](https://wiki.gnuradio.org/index.php?title=Tutorials) — GNU Radio Tutorials
- [SRC-006](https://sigmf.org/) — Signal Metadata Format Specification

## Applied work
Sketch two synthetic waves and explain why modulation does not reveal an owner.

## Review question
**Can a spectrogram establish institutional attribution alone?** Answer in 120–250 words, including one source ID and one limitation of your answer.

## Evidence requirement
Do not fabricate measurements or citations. Label simulated data. For institutional claims, state whether the source is the institution itself or an external assessment. If evidence is missing, mark the question as unresolved.
