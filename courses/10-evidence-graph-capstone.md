# Lesson 10 — Evidence-graph capstone

**Learning objective:** Build a traceable, bounded claim without filling gaps with guesswork.

**Pre-class reading:** [docs/military/05_source_fusion.md](../docs/military/05_source_fusion.md)

## Core concepts
Read the pre-class chapter and identify three carefully defined terms. Check them against the official sources, rather than a model-generated summary. Distinguish an objective physical principle from an institutional claim or legal assertion.

## Recommended sources
- [SRC-011](https://www.asd.gov.au/about/what-we-do/signals-intelligence) — Signals Intelligence
- [SRC-017](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/drm/actualites/esprit-defense-bataille-invisible-du-renseignement) — La bataille invisible du renseignement
- [SRC-021](https://nato.int/en/what-we-do/deterrence-and-defence/joint-intelligence-surveillance-and-reconnaissance) — Joint Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance

## Applied work
Write one evidence-backed claim; add provenance, alternatives, and two open questions.

## Review question
**Can three documents repeating one report count as three independent confirmations?** Answer in 120–250 words, including one source ID and one limitation of your answer.

## Evidence requirement
Do not fabricate measurements or citations. Label simulated data. For institutional claims, state whether the source is the institution itself or an external assessment. If evidence is missing, mark the question as unresolved.
