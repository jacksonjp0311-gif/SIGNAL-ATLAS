# Lesson 04 — Sensors and receivers

**Learning objective:** Outline antenna, filtering, conversion, sampling and noise.

**Pre-class reading:** [docs/technology/README.md](../docs/technology/README.md)

## Core concepts
Read the pre-class chapter and identify three carefully defined terms. Check them against the official sources, rather than a model-generated summary. Distinguish an objective physical principle from an institutional claim or legal assertion.

## Recommended sources
- [SRC-005](https://wiki.gnuradio.org/index.php?title=Tutorials) — GNU Radio Tutorials
- [SRC-008](https://www.nasa.gov/smallsat-institute/sst-soa/soa-communications/) — Small Satellite Communications (State of the Art)

## Applied work
Diagram a receiver using only synthetic tones; identify sampling and metadata fields.

## Review question
**What is the difference between the waveform and an interpretation of it?** Answer in 120–250 words, including one source ID and one limitation of your answer.

## Evidence requirement
Do not fabricate measurements or citations. Label simulated data. For institutional claims, state whether the source is the institution itself or an external assessment. If evidence is missing, mark the question as unresolved.
