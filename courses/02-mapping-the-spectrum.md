# Lesson 02 — Mapping the spectrum

**Learning objective:** Interpret band nomenclature and separate spectrum, allocation and assignment.

**Pre-class reading:** [docs/spectrum/03_allocation_vs_observation.md](../docs/spectrum/03_allocation_vs_observation.md)

## Core concepts
Read the pre-class chapter and identify three carefully defined terms. Check them against the official sources, rather than a model-generated summary. Distinguish an objective physical principle from an institutional claim or legal assertion.

## Recommended sources
- [SRC-003](https://www.ntia.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/ntia-us-frequency-allocations.pdf) — United States Frequency Allocations (September 2025)
- [SRC-004](https://www.itu.int/pub/R-REG/en) — ITU Radio Regulations and allocation tools

## Applied work
Make a two-column list of physical facts versus legal/regulatory facts.

## Review question
**Is a national allocation chart an active radio census?** Answer in 120–250 words, including one source ID and one limitation of your answer.

## Evidence requirement
Do not fabricate measurements or citations. Label simulated data. For institutional claims, state whether the source is the institution itself or an external assessment. If evidence is missing, mark the question as unresolved.
