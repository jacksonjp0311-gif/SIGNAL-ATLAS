# Lesson 07 — Published military missions around the world

**Learning objective:** Compare published SIGINT roles across countries with attribution.

**Pre-class reading:** [docs/military/04_global_survey.md](../docs/military/04_global_survey.md)

## Core concepts
Read the pre-class chapter and identify three carefully defined terms. Check them against the official sources, rather than a model-generated summary. Distinguish an objective physical principle from an institutional claim or legal assertion.

## Recommended sources
- [SRC-013](https://www.gchq.gov.uk/section/mission/overview) — Our Mission
- [SRC-012](https://www.cse-cst.gc.ca/en/mission/foreign-intelligence) — Foreign Intelligence
- [SRC-015](https://fra.se/system/engelska/english.4.55af049f184e92956c42ca2.html) — FRA English Summary
- [SRC-017](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/drm/actualites/esprit-defense-bataille-invisible-du-renseignement) — La bataille invisible du renseignement

## Applied work
Compare two countries using only source-attributed, dated public mandates.

## Review question
**What can institutional self-description establish, and what can it not?** Answer in 120–250 words, including one source ID and one limitation of your answer.

## Evidence requirement
Do not fabricate measurements or citations. Label simulated data. For institutional claims, state whether the source is the institution itself or an external assessment. If evidence is missing, mark the question as unresolved.
