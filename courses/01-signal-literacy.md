# Lesson 01 — Signal literacy

**Learning objective:** Define a signal, electromagnetic wave, frequency, amplitude and unit.

**Pre-class reading:** [docs/spectrum/01_waves_and_units.md](../docs/spectrum/01_waves_and_units.md)

## Core concepts
Read the pre-class chapter and identify three carefully defined terms. Check them against the official sources, rather than a model-generated summary. Distinguish an objective physical principle from an institutional claim or legal assertion.

## Recommended sources
- [SRC-001](https://science.nasa.gov/ems/01_intro/) — Introduction to the Electromagnetic Spectrum
- [SRC-002](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/somd/space-communications-navigation-program/spectrum-overview/) — Spectrum Overview

## Applied work
Compute 3 wavelengths and label uncertainty about propagation medium.

## Review question
**Does frequency identify a transmitter? Why not?** Answer in 120–250 words, including one source ID and one limitation of your answer.

## Evidence requirement
Do not fabricate measurements or citations. Label simulated data. For institutional claims, state whether the source is the institution itself or an external assessment. If evidence is missing, mark the question as unresolved.
