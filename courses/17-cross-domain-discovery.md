# Cross-domain scientific hypothesis studio

**Goal:** Build a candidate insight with falsifiable prediction, source IDs and controls.

**Prerequisites:** lessons 01-05 and the [Science Division](../docs/science/README.md).

## Theory and teaching material

Read the [chapter](../docs/science/03_knowledge_discovery.md) and explore the related diagrams in [the visual library](../assets/README.md). The new [document library](../documents/INDEX.md) links relevant research papers and original downloadable PDF briefs.

## Safe laboratory assignment

Construct a one-page hypothesis card connecting two scientific papers, with an explicit invariant, negative control, alternative explanation and reproducibility procedure. Keep it in the Research Desk until reviewed.

## Assessment

Submit (1) one correct dimensional equation, (2) at least one source ID and URL, (3) assumption/limitation notes, and (4) a clearly described result. Distinguish observed results from predictions. Do not use private or operational communications as data.
