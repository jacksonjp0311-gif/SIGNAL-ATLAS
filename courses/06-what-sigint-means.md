# Lesson 06 — What SIGINT means

**Learning objective:** Distinguish COMINT, ELINT, FISINT, OSINT, ISR and EMW.

**Pre-class reading:** [docs/military/01_definitions.md](../docs/military/01_definitions.md)

## Core concepts
Read the pre-class chapter and identify three carefully defined terms. Check them against the official sources, rather than a model-generated summary. Distinguish an objective physical principle from an institutional claim or legal assertion.

## Recommended sources
- [SRC-009](https://www.nsa.gov/Signals-Intelligence/Overview/) — Signals Intelligence Overview
- [SRC-011](https://www.asd.gov.au/about/what-we-do/signals-intelligence) — Signals Intelligence
- [SRC-020](https://www.nato.int/en/what-we-do/deterrence-and-defence/electromagnetic-warfare) — Electromagnetic Warfare

## Applied work
Construct a concept map showing overlap and clear boundaries.

## Review question
**Are SIGINT and electronic warfare synonyms?** Answer in 120–250 words, including one source ID and one limitation of your answer.

## Evidence requirement
Do not fabricate measurements or citations. Label simulated data. For institutional claims, state whether the source is the institution itself or an external assessment. If evidence is missing, mark the question as unresolved.
