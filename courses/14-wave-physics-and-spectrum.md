# Wave physics and spectral scale

**Goal:** Distinguish physical waves, information-bearing signals, frequency labels and measurements.

**Prerequisites:** lessons 01-05 and the [Science Division](../docs/science/README.md).

## Theory and teaching material

Read the [chapter](../docs/science/01_wave_physics.md) and explore the related diagrams in [the visual library](../assets/README.md). The new [document library](../documents/INDEX.md) links relevant research papers and original downloadable PDF briefs.

## Safe laboratory assignment

Study `lambda = c/f` and explain how material media alter wave speed. Compute wavelengths for three publicly allocated *educational* frequencies. Use the new wave scale diagram and explain why a spectrum allocation is not an observed transmission.

## Assessment

Submit (1) one correct dimensional equation, (2) at least one source ID and URL, (3) assumption/limitation notes, and (4) a clearly described result. Distinguish observed results from predictions. Do not use private or operational communications as data.
