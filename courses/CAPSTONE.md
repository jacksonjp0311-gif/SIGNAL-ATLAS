# Capstone — A source-grounded SIGINT knowledge trail

## Scenario
A museum educator asks: *How does public documentation describe the relationship between electromagnetic science, signals intelligence and military decision-making in at least two countries?*

## Deliverables

1. **Concept map** connecting electromagnetic signals, SIGINT subtypes, military decision-making, official doctrine and international regulatory context.
2. **Comparison table** for at least two published country/institution profiles with exact source IDs and links.
3. **One defensible claim** and an attribution note explaining what is directly documented versus unproven.
4. **Two gaps** that could be researched next using only lawful public documentation.
5. **Rights note**: identify what could be linked versus what could be redistributed.

## Rubric (100 points)
Source identification and provenance 25; technical accuracy 20; attribution of country-level claims 20; honest uncertainty 20; navigation/clarity 15.

## Disallowed shortcuts
No invented sources, covert operational estimates, tactical frequency catalogs, private intercepted data, extrapolated emitter locations, or unlicensed full-page copying. Deep research can be valuable without these activities.
