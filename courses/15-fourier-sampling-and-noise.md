# Fourier, sampling and noise

**Goal:** Explain Fourier representations and demonstrate sampling aliases with synthetic data.

**Prerequisites:** lessons 01-05 and the [Science Division](../docs/science/README.md).

## Theory and teaching material

Read the [chapter](../docs/science/02_signal_mathematics.md) and explore the related diagrams in [the visual library](../assets/README.md). The new [document library](../documents/INDEX.md) links relevant research papers and original downloadable PDF briefs.

## Safe laboratory assignment

Generate 7 Hz and 3 Hz numerical sine waves at a 10 Hz sample rate. Demonstrate an alias. Re-sample at 30 Hz and describe the difference. Identify what phase, noise, filtering and the assumed bandwidth do to your result.

## Assessment

Submit (1) one correct dimensional equation, (2) at least one source ID and URL, (3) assumption/limitation notes, and (4) a clearly described result. Distinguish observed results from predictions. Do not use private or operational communications as data.
