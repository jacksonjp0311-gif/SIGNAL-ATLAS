# Instructor and evaluator notes

A successful learner can describe wavelength without confusing it with signal meaning, separate allocation from observation, differentiate COMINT/ELINT/FISINT without overclaiming taxonomy universality, and distinguish the evidence status of a national agency statement from third-party assessments.

Suggested formative checks: short oral explanations; two-source comparison; spectrum terminology quiz; reproducibility check of a fully synthetic dataset; peer review of one `claims.jsonl` entry.

**AI evaluation:** ask for a country comparison and reject outputs that attribute one outside government's report to the assessed government. Demand explicit source IDs and nonclaims. The highest-scoring answer is not the longest but the most traceable.
