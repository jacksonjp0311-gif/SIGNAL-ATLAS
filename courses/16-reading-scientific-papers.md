# How to read a scientific paper

**Goal:** Differentiate physics principles, mathematical assumptions, author results, reproducibility, and consensus.

**Prerequisites:** lessons 01-05 and the [Science Division](../docs/science/README.md).

## Theory and teaching material

Read the [chapter](../docs/science/04_literature_map.md) and explore the related diagrams in [the visual library](../assets/README.md). The new [document library](../documents/INDEX.md) links relevant research papers and original downloadable PDF briefs.

## Safe laboratory assignment

Read the original record of PUB-002 and PUB-003. Extract a theorem or empirical claim from each, name the assumptions and determine whether the evidence is mathematical, simulated or measured. Do not claim reproduction without running it.

## Assessment

Submit (1) one correct dimensional equation, (2) at least one source ID and URL, (3) assumption/limitation notes, and (4) a clearly described result. Distinguish observed results from predictions. Do not use private or operational communications as data.
