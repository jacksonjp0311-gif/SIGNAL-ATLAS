# Lesson 09 — Historical intelligence and source criticism

**Learning objective:** Analyze historical radio intelligence and evidence bias.

**Pre-class reading:** [docs/military/06_history.md](../docs/military/06_history.md)

## Core concepts
Read the pre-class chapter and identify three carefully defined terms. Check them against the official sources, rather than a model-generated summary. Distinguish an objective physical principle from an institutional claim or legal assertion.

## Recommended sources
- [SRC-025](https://www.history.navy.mil/research/library/bibliographies-and-research-guides/research-guides/cryptologic-documents.html) — Cryptologic Documents Collection
- [SRC-026](https://www.history.navy.mil/research/library/online-reading-room/title-list-alphabetically/b/a-brief-history-of-naval-cryptanalysis.html) — A Brief History of Naval Cryptanalysis
- [SRC-030](https://www.archives.gov/research/intelligence/nsa) — Records of the Central Intelligence Community

## Applied work
Compare two historical documents by date, author, purpose and limits.

## Review question
**Why is a wartime source not proof of current capabilities?** Answer in 120–250 words, including one source ID and one limitation of your answer.

## Evidence requirement
Do not fabricate measurements or citations. Label simulated data. For institutional claims, state whether the source is the institution itself or an external assessment. If evidence is missing, mark the question as unresolved.
