# Military signal intelligence: historical exploration

Recommended research arc:

**1 — Pre-radio and early wireless:** why interception and cryptology became important after widespread use of radio. Start at SRC-026 (US Naval History).

**2 — World War I and World War II:** evaluate declassified documents such as the Naval History and Heritage Command cryptologic guide SRC-025. Compare primary documents with later historical synthesis, and record publication provenance.

**3 — Cold War:** focus on public institutional histories, oversight and technological transformation using SRC-030 and SRC-031. Do not convert historical examples into present-day capabilities without new evidence.

**4 — Contemporary doctrine:** review high-level NATO EMW and JISR frameworks (SRC-020 and SRC-021), distinguishing historical evolution from present mandates.

**Research exercise:** select two declassified historical accounts, compare their date and author, and explain one plausible source of bias. Do not reconstruct active networks or publish private intercepted material.
