# Military context — what public documents establish

Worldwide, published military and intelligence frameworks identify broad uses of SIGINT in:

- **Strategic awareness:** informing national leaders about foreign developments and risks.
- **Defense and force protection:** providing commanders and policy staff with interpreted information.
- **Multi-domain intelligence fusion:** combining information from electromagnetic, imagery, geographic, human and other source families.
- **Historical cryptologic work:** understanding radio communications and cryptologic intelligence in past wars.
- **Spectrum and communications security:** understanding interference, radio operations and protection of communications.

Public examples: NSA describes support to policymakers and military forces (SRC-009); CSE describes defense support (SRC-012); NATO describes ISR fusion for decision-makers (SRC-021); France describes the integration of multiple intelligence sources (SRC-017).

**What this does not establish:** operational reach, signal frequencies, current target lists, active sensor locations, staffing levels, classified interception techniques, or independently measured effectiveness.

A useful comparative template is *organization → published legal mandate → published collection discipline → analysis role → dissemination relationship → oversight → historical examples → evidence limitations*.
