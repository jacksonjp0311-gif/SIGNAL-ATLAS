# SIGINT categories and adjacent disciplines

**SIGINT (signals intelligence)** is intelligence derived from electronic signals. Official public sources divide it into several disciplines, commonly **COMINT**, **ELINT** and **FISINT**. In the public ASD taxonomy (SRC-011):

- **COMINT:** intelligence from communications signals (as opposed to the intended recipient's ordinary reception).
- **ELINT:** intelligence from noncommunications electromagnetic emissions, often radar-related.
- **FISINT:** intelligence from foreign instrumentation signals, historically including technical telemetry.

**OSINT** is research from publicly available sources. This *library* uses OSINT methods **to study SIGINT**, but it does not collect SIGINT itself.

**ISR** combines intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance across sensors and information sources. **EMW/EW** is an operational military discipline involving use of electromagnetic energy for awareness, defensive and offensive effects (SRC-020). It overlaps SIGINT conceptually but is not a synonym.

**Analytical warning:** classification terms vary by country, era and source. Do not force perfect equivalence. Reference: SRC-011, SRC-009, SRC-020, SRC-021.
