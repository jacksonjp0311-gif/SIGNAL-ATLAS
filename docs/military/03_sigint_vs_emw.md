# SIGINT vs electromagnetic warfare (EMW)

| Term | Central question | Typical documentary source |
|---|---|---|
| SIGINT | What intelligence can be assessed from electronic emissions? | NSA, ASD, CSE public explanations |
| EMW/EW | How can militaries use electromagnetic energy for operational awareness or effects? | NATO EMW overview |
| Spectrum management | Who may use which radio service, under what regulations? | ITU / NTIA |
| Electronic engineering | How is a waveform generated, measured and processed? | NASA / GNU Radio / standards |
| OSINT research | What can we establish using lawful public documentary material? | Archives, published mandates, official reports |

SIGINT and EMW may share relevant electromagnetic phenomena without being the same discipline or collection authority. Educational analysis here remains at the high-level published-doctrine and historical level; this page is not an operational procedure.

Primary material: SRC-020, SRC-021, SRC-011, SRC-004.
