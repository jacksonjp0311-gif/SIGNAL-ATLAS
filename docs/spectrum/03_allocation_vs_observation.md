# Allocation ≠ assignment ≠ observation

- **Physical band:** range of electromagnetic frequencies, independent of political borders.
- **Allocation:** a regulatory designation for one or more radio services in a range (ITU/national).
- **Assignment/license:** specific authorization, commonly with geographic and technical conditions.
- **Observation:** an actual measurement from a receiver or instrument at a given time.
- **Intelligence assessment:** interpretation, which may require multiple streams of evidence.

For a national allocation chart, use SRC-003 as an American example; for international regulations see SRC-004. Neither gives an inventory of operational military transmissions or allows inference of a particular unit's location.
