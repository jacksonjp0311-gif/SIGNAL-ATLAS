# Published research mapped to the physics

The document library is intentionally arranged by underlying **question**, not just title or institution. For every item, open its [publication card](../../documents/INDEX.md), then the original paper.

| Theme | Scientific question | References |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field propagation | How do Maxwell constraints influence RF models? | PUB-004, PUB-005, PUB-007 |
| Sensing + communications | How can two inference objectives share one physical model? | PUB-001 |
| Time-frequency estimation | What information changes with STFT window length? | PUB-002 |
| Sampling and reconstruction | When can fewer measurements recover a signal? | PUB-003, PUB-008, PUB-009 |
| Spectrum sensing methods | How are measurement and model limits reported? | PUB-010, PUB-011 |
| ML RF representations | Do learned features generalize to unseen data? | PUB-006 |
| Formal provenance | What evidence and transformations produced an answer? | PUB-013 |

**Reading order:** establish elementary physics, examine the published assumptions, extract the reproducible result, then compare distinct papers. A theoretical paper and an engineering benchmark are not interchangeable forms of evidence. A recent preprint is not automatically a consensus result.
