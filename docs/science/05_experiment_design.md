# Scientific experiments and claim admission

A claim may move from **candidate -> reproduced -> independently corroborated -> accepted with scope** only through an explicit review. A dataset or equations alone do not justify automated promotion.

## Protocol

1. State an answerable hypothesis with a defined falsifier.
2. Locate original source version and exact equation/page; record license.
3. Reproduce the published method under its stated assumptions.
4. Build negative controls and alternative explanations.
5. Measure effect size, uncertainty, latency and compute where relevant.
6. Re-run on a fresh independent test set; compare to a simple baseline.
7. Publish both positive and negative results with dataset hash and experiment recipe.
8. Accept only after a human source-evidence review and a separate agent retrieval test.

## Example: aliasing laboratory

Generate a 7 Hz sinusoid and sample at 10 samples per second for 5 seconds. Compare it against a 3 Hz sinusoid at the same sample times. Explain the sign convention and integer-period dependence. Then repeat at 30 samples per second with identical phase conventions: the ambiguity disappears for the two tested frequencies.

**Do not generalize** this exercise to live RF collection or identifying people/systems. Use synthetic or authorized laboratory data.
