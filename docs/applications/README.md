# Application atlas

The science and learning methods transfer across many **lawful and non-sensitive** applications:

- **Space science:** RF and optical communication, satellite links and open scientific observations (NASA SRC-002, SRC-008).
- **Meteorology and Earth observation:** interpreting public satellite datasets and understanding links; verify the specific data's license.
- **Amateur radio and education:** spectrum literacy, modulation, propagation and instrument experiments within relevant licensing rules.
- **Industrial reliability:** monitoring the RF environment and electromagnetic compatibility in an owned/test environment (authorized equipment only).
- **Digital signal processing:** supervised experiments with generated audio or IQ samples (GNU Radio SRC-005, SigMF SRC-006).
- **Military history and ethics:** critically analyzing published SIGINT/cryptologic narratives and archival records (SRC-025, SRC-026).
- **International law and governance:** compare official mandates and oversight principles (SRC-010, SRC-033, SRC-029).
- **Spectrum policy:** contrast international radio allocations with US examples (SRC-004, SRC-003).

## Example cross-disciplinary trail

A question about **satellite communications** leads from electromagnetic waves → RF frequency bands → antennas → encoding and modulation → metadata standards → SatNOGS documentation → published regulatory constraints. The same physics can be studied without attempting to intercept protected systems.
