# NATO — public SIGINT research profile

**Published institution / framework:** Alliance-level doctrine and ISR frameworks  
**Seed evidence:** Alliance is not a country. Official frameworks for EM warfare and intelligence fusion.

## Original sources
- **SRC-020** [Electromagnetic Warfare](https://www.nato.int/en/what-we-do/deterrence-and-defence/electromagnetic-warfare) — High-level official distinction between electromagnetic operations and electromagnetic warfare.
- **SRC-021** [Joint Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance](https://nato.int/en/what-we-do/deterrence-and-defence/joint-intelligence-surveillance-and-reconnaissance) — Official NATO description of intelligence fusion and alliance information sharing, updated July 2026.
- **SRC-036** [NATO Joint ISR Service Portfolio](https://www.ncia.nato.int/about-us/service-portfolio/joint-intelligence-surveillance-and-reconnaissance) — Public description of cross-national ISR information services and data integration.

## Established scope
The above items are public **research entrypoints**. They can document the nature of published roles, institutional context, and historical or doctrinal descriptions, subject to source dates and limitations.

## Uncertainty / attribution
Published self-description may not be an independent verification of operational effectiveness.

## Open scholarly questions
- Which statutes, parliamentary/public oversight reports or independent histories deepen the published picture?
- How does the available doctrine define collection, analysis, defense support and legal limitations?
- Which materials are historical, outdated, retracted, replaced or incomplete?

**Boundary:** no cataloging of current unit positions, live emitter frequencies, private intercepted traffic or tactical targeting data.
