# France — public SIGINT research profile

**Published institution / framework:** DGSE; Directorate of Military Intelligence (DRM)  
**Seed evidence:** Separate external and military public references; multi-source intelligence fusion.

## Original sources
- **SRC-016** [Who Are We?](https://www.dgse.gouv.fr/en/get-to-know-us/who-are-we) — France external intelligence service descriptions, including technical intelligence and support.
- **SRC-017** [La bataille invisible du renseignement](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/drm/actualites/esprit-defense-bataille-invisible-du-renseignement) — French military intelligence discussion of fusion across electromagnetic, imagery, cyber, human, and partner sources.

## Established scope
The above items are public **research entrypoints**. They can document the nature of published roles, institutional context, and historical or doctrinal descriptions, subject to source dates and limitations.

## Uncertainty / attribution
Published self-description may not be an independent verification of operational effectiveness.

## Open scholarly questions
- Which statutes, parliamentary/public oversight reports or independent histories deepen the published picture?
- How does the available doctrine define collection, analysis, defense support and legal limitations?
- Which materials are historical, outdated, retracted, replaced or incomplete?

**Boundary:** no cataloging of current unit positions, live emitter frequencies, private intercepted traffic or tactical targeting data.
