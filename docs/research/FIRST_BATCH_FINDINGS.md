# Editorial analysis of discovery batch B

**Date:** 2026-11-02 | **Records newly indexed:** 40 | **New bounded claims:** 18 | **Research notes:** 8

## Finding A: International common language has limits

French [military DRM public material](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/drm/actualites/serie-zoom-renseignement-dorigine-electromagnetique-45) explains ROEM as electromagnetic-origin intelligence. Australian [ASD SIGINT explainer](https://www.asd.gov.au/about/what-we-do/signals-intelligence) distinguishes COMINT, ELINT and FISINT. Use these to teach shared categories, but do not assume equivalent mandates, capabilities or definitions across countries.

## Finding B: Archives offer a way to substantiate history

The [UK National Archives guide](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/intelligence-and-security-services/) points researchers to HW and DEFE series. The [NSA historical index](https://www.nsa.gov/serve-from-netstorage/History/Cryptologic-History/Historical-Publications/index.html) and [ASD declassified holdings](https://www.asd.gov.au/about/history/declassified) offer national historical material. This enables contextual source comparison, not direct inference about present intelligence assets.

## Finding C: Research infrastructure should carry machine-readable provenance

[PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) documents how activities and agents produced records; [DCAT 3](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-dcat-3/) organizes resource catalogs; [SKOS](https://www.w3.org/TR/skos-reference/) encodes subject terms and relations. These are complementary design references, not yet formally implemented ontology exports.

## Finding D: Spectrum expertise should remain separable from military doctrine

[ITU 2026 spectrum-monitoring resources](https://www.itu.int/pub/R-HDB-23) and [NTIA distributed monitoring research](https://its.ntia.gov/research/rfm/spectrum-monitoring) cover public spectrum-engineering work. They are relevant to sampling, systems engineering and lawful monitoring, but do not justify cataloging active military emitters.

## Caution

These notes are first-pass editorial syntheses of linked pages. We have not performed a systematic literature review or independent technical experiments to substantiate claims about military effectiveness. Readers and agents should open the original source before drawing further conclusions.
