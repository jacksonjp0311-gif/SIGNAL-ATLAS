## Research decisions

- Promote new source cards as **indexed references only**; use `source-attributed` for new claims.
- Keep cross-domain syntheses in the Research Desk as candidates.
- Link third-party PDF/technical document pages; include no copied external PDFs.
- Use new world pages to illustrate documentary evidence classes, not operational capability comparisons.
- Ensure a human can reach every new source from the offline explorer and course pages.

## Gaps

Most scholarly papers are *metadata-level* discoveries. Independent page/equation verification and meaningful human/AI comprehension tests remain pending. New countries have one to three primary sources, not comprehensive country coverage.
