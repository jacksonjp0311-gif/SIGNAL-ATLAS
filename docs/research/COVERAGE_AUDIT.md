## Research expansion priorities

1. Improve primary-language official and academic coverage of Africa, Latin America, South Asia, Southeast Asia and the Middle East without collecting current tactical emission details.
2. Read specific historical archive documents rather than relying solely on archival catalog descriptions.
3. Find genuinely independent corroboration rather than multiple sites repeating agency self-reports.
4. Expand synthetic engineering illustrations for modulation, propagation, signal metadata and measurement uncertainty.
5. Perform explicit per-source rights decisions before copying full-text pages, PDFs, images, or datasets.
6. Test human comprehension and mobile/keyboard navigation with independent readers, not just automated link integrity.
