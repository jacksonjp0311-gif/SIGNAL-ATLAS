## Improvements

- 3 additional country/context pages: Finland (official military review), Brazil (civilian intelligence institutional history only), South Africa (general defense reporting only).
- Additional original-language Japanese defense policy and Brazilian Portuguese institutional records.
- A new independent New Zealand oversight archive provides a distinct evidence type from agency self-description.

## Still missing

- Large geographic imbalance; many countries have only one or two catalog cards.
- Most pages have not been line-verified and scholarly preprints were mostly read at abstract/metadata level.
- Many regional claims remain uncorroborated by independent, contemporaneous primary-source records.
- No comparative inventory of current tactical emitters, collection locations, signals or interception opportunities is included.

## Next audit

Rate evidence depth and independence per *claim*, not just count of source URLs. Also evaluate original-language retrieval fidelity and human comprehension.
