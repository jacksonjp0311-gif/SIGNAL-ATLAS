## Coverage gaps

- Mathematical signal theory: principles indexed, but full equation-level reviews pending.
- EM propagation: need more primary sources and experimental material/dispersion measurements.
- Optical, acoustic, seismic and biomedical signals: currently sparse; extend as separate tracks with their own units and safety contexts.
- English-language discovery bias: increase non-English scientific source records and review their translations.
- Reproduction: no original-paper experimental performance results have yet been independently replicated by this package.
- Research novelty: no newly discovered physical law or verified cross-domain result has been established; all discovery notes are candidates.
