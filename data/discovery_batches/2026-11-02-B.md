# Discovery batch B — 2 November 2026

This batch **adds 40 curated bibliographic source cards**, not full copyrighted article or PDF copies. Web-discovered official landing pages and described materials were checked at discovery level, not exhaustively audited. The titles, descriptions and classifications are new Atlas metadata; linked source content remains on its original host.

## Pipeline
- Query primary, standards, university, archival, and oversight collections.
- Record stable source ID and canonical URL; reject URL duplicates.
- Capture original short summary with country, subject, language and time scope.
- Add specific attributed claims and identify evidence limitations.
- Link new material into research courses, country profiles, and science articles.
- Preserve research gaps rather than extrapolating unknown capabilities.

## Status and rights
- `review_status: landing-page-checked` means the landing page or search description was examined, **not** that the full document, site terms, or all historical claims were independently verified.
- `snapshot_status: catalog-card-only` means no third-party source text has been copied.
- All original source URLs, with identifiers, are in `data/sources.jsonl`.
- No automatic crawler has been run and there is no schedule running.

## Review queue
1. Manually verify every new source URL, date, language, author, licensing terms and stable identifier.
2. For archival PDFs, inspect the actual historical document before writing page-specific findings.
3. Request independent corroboration before promoting comparative or contested interpretations.
4. User-test pathways for beginners and historians with people unfamiliar with the repository.
