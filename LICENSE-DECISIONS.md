# Licensing decisions before public release

This is a draft artifact. The publisher should decide the license for the original essays, diagrams and catalog data. Possible choices: CC BY 4.0 for authored text/graphics, CC0 or ODC-BY for original metadata, and a standard license for any future software. **No third-party source inherits our license**. Government documents, logos and diagrams are handled separately under the source's applicable terms. Do not publish scraped full text by default.
