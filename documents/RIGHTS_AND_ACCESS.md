# Access is not redistribution

Scientific facts, mathematical relationships and hypotheses may be described and independently explained, with citations. A publisher's wording, figures and full paper PDF have separate rights. We include external citation metadata and direct downloads for discovery regardless of license; we redistribute full third-party PDFs **only after verifying a license that permits the intended sharing**, with authorship and license preserved.

- `CC BY 4.0`: eligible to share with attribution, license URL and change notice; check individual paper and any independently sourced contents.
- `arXiv non-exclusive`: free on arXiv, but **not freely redistributable by Signal Atlas**; link to source PDF.
- `unverified`: link-only until reviewed.
- `CC BY-NC-SA`: do not casually bundle with a freely reusable/commercial repository without observing the specific license conditions.
- Public scientific knowledge can be indexed, explained and cross-referenced even when a paper is link-only.

Never remove original attribution, bypass publisher access controls, collect private communications, or confuse a research preprint with peer-reviewed consensus. See https://info.arxiv.org/help/license/index.html and https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/ .
